How to Play

✊✋✌️

Every round, all players play against one dealer throw. Pick your hand, then see if you beat the dealer.

Playing a Round

  1. Pick ✊ Rock, ✋ Paper, or ✌️ Scissors — your bet places instantly.
  2. Watch the timer — when it ends, the dealer reveals one hand for everyone.
  3. Beat the dealer's hand to win the round.

Reading the Screen

  • Dealer row: the dealer's next and past moves.
  • Player row: ❓is your next move, and your own past moves, lined up under the dealer's so you can compare them round by round.
  • Heatmap (% bar): what other players are betting on right now.
  • Players list: shows other players Activities.
  • Auto Bet plays hands-free for you.
